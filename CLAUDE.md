# Pixel Transport

## Game design rules

- **Every job type needs a cheap carrier in every category.** When a new job type (like fuel or bulk) is added, or the level it starts spawning at changes, make sure that at or before that level the shop has a small, cheap vehicle that can carry it in **each category that can reach the towns spawning it**: a train car, a truck (or trailer for the Semi), a boat and a plane. Otherwise a town (especially an island with only a port or airport) fills up with jobs nothing can ship.
  - Current examples: Hopper car / Dump trailer / Barge / Bushhawk for bulk (level 4), Tanker / Tank trailer or Fuel Truck / Bunker Boat / Firefly for fuel (level 6), Mail car / Mail Van / Coaster / Sparrow for mail (level 2), Vault car / Armored Car / Courier Launch / Courier Jet for valuables (level 11). The Semi (level 2) is the cheap tractor that pulls those trailers.
  - Exception: containers (level 7, `CONTAINER` in `src/game/data.js`) don't fly, so they only need the Container flatcar, the Container chassis trailer (pulled by the Semi) and the Feeder Ship / Container barge. They start and end at stations and ports, and pay `CONTAINER.bonus` more for each extra kind of vehicle that carries them (`j.via`, recorded in `dropOff`).
  - Exception: wide loads (level 8) only move overland, between mainland towns on the same landmass, so they only need the Lowboy trailer (pulled by the Semi) and the Flatcar.
  - Superloads (level 9+, contract only) go by road on the Superload Tractor, by rail on the Heavy Haul Engine, or by sea on the Heavy Lift Ship (`by` in `SUPERLOAD.loads`), and each brings its own art (`SUPERLOAD_TRAILERS` in `src/game/vehicles.js`). Each also needs a set of escort vehicles: by road the Pilot Car, Police Car, Push Truck, Bucket Truck and Fire Truck; by rail the Helper Engine and Track Inspection Car; by sea the Tugboat, Pilot Boat and Fireboat. A superload's `level` in `SUPERLOAD.loads` (`src/game/data.js`) must be at least its carrier's level and the level of every escort it `needs`.
  - Exception: tourists (level 12) only ride show-off vehicles (models with `tour` capacity: the Grand Tourer, Royal Express, Mach Dart and Wonder of the Waves megaship). That's on purpose, so they're priced as money sinks rather than cheap. Tourists only turn up once the fleet has one, and they leave after `TOURISTS.ttl`, so they can't clog a town.
  - Megaprojects (`PROJECTS` in `src/game/data.js`): a project's `level` must be at least the level of every job type in its `needs` (`TYPE_LEVEL` in `src/game/game.svelte.js`) and of every superload in its `supers`. A bridge's `link` towns must be mainland towns with roads and a station.
  - Job spawn levels are in `makeJob` in `src/game/game.svelte.js`; vehicle levels and capacities are in `src/game/vehicles.js`.
- **Map coordinates are written unscaled.** Town positions, anchorages and continent spines in `src/game/data.js` are for a 960×640 map and get multiplied by `SCALE`, as do plane `range`s in `src/game/vehicles.js`. Write any new distance in world units (a range, a radius, how far jobs travel) for the unscaled map and multiply it by `SCALE`. Leave art-sized things (a town's footprint, road offsets, tree spacing) unscaled.
